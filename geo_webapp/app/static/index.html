Record or upload some Bangla speech; 10 to 20 seconds works best. The model estimates the speaker's regional dialect, gender and age group, and says when it is unsure.

        What it can and cannot do

        Dialect of the nine RBVD speakers

        Reliable. On sentences it never heard from these speakers, the dialect macro-F1 is 0.983.

        Dialect of a new speaker

        Not reliable. Each RBVD district was recorded by one person, so the model learned to recognise those voices rather than the dialects. A held-out Rangpur speaker was recognised as Rangpur in 0% of clips. When a voice is unlike all nine, this page says Unknown voice.

        Gender and age group

        Tested on Common Voice speakers the model never heard: gender 94.3% accuracy, age group balanced accuracy 0.617. Common Voice Bangla has no labelled speakers aged 60 or over, so age has two groups. Recordings made in very different conditions may score lower.

        Model card

        A ResNet-50 reads the log-mel spectrogram of each 5-second window and a two-layer Bi-LSTM reads its MFCC sequence. Their embeddings are joined in one shared layer that feeds three heads. Dialect was learned from the Regional Bangla Voice Dataset (RBVD, 1,067 clips, 8 districts); gender and age from Mozilla Common Voice Bangla. No gender or age label was taken from RBVD.
